About Mike Charlie

Who is Mike Charlie?

Mike Charlie is a Filipino slow-living and eco-conscious brand founded in 2018, born from a personal journey toward more intentional, sustainable living. The name comes from my late father's radio call sign for my mother, using her initials in the phonetic alphabet.

What began as a small online shop has grown into a creative lifestyle brand built around sustainability, thoughtful design, and intentional everyday living. Today, Mike Charlie creates products and experiences that bring these values into homes, travels, and everyday spaces.
