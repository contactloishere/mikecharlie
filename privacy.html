Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Mike Charlie Co. is a small family business in the Philippines. This page explains, in plain words, what personal information we collect when you shop with us or sign in, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Your contact details: your name, delivery address, phone number, and your chat handle (for example iMessage, WhatsApp, Viber, Threads or Instagram).
  • Your account details, if you make an account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Your orders: the items you ordered, your shipping region, your payment method, and the screenshot you upload as proof of payment.
  • Your hearts: the products you save, if you are logged in.

Why we collect it

  • To pack and deliver your order.
  • To confirm your payment.
  • To message you about your order through the chat app you chose.
  • To show your order progress and history in your account.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Couriers (SPX or J&T) receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver your parcel.
  • Our service providers help us run the shop. Your information is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and file storage. Our website is hosted on Vercel. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name and email address.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to handle your order, answer questions about it, and meet our business and tax record-keeping needs. If you have an account, we keep it until you ask us to delete it.

How we protect it

Your information is stored in a protected database. Logged-in customers can only see their own orders. Only the shop owner can see all orders. No system is perfectly safe, but we take care to limit who can see what.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you may ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete your account and personal information, and
  • stop using your information for a purpose you did not agree to.

Some order records may need to be kept for a time for legal or accounting reasons. We will tell you if that applies.

Cookies and device storage

We save a few things on your device so the shop works: your tote bag, your hearted products, and your login, so you stay signed in. You can clear these any time from your browser settings, or tap Log Out.
